PROJECT 01 / MANAGERIAL ACCOUNTING

Job Costing &
Financial Analysis.

An Excel-based study of job-costing data, focused on identifying how revenue, costs, and overhead affect performance across products and regions.

CONTEXTIntro to Managerial Accounting
WHENSpring 2026
METHODSExcel formulas, PivotTables, variance analysis
THE PROJECT / 01

The question

How can raw job-costing data become a clearer picture of product and regional performance? This project brought together revenue, gross margin, direct material, direct labor, and manufacturing overhead for structured analysis.

What I did

  • Used Excel formulas and PivotTables to organize and compare job-costing data across products and geographic regions.
  • Examined cost and variance measures, including applied versus actual manufacturing overhead and direct-material price and efficiency variances.
  • Considered contribution margins and break-even performance to understand the implications of different cost patterns.

What it demonstrates

The work shows a disciplined path from transaction-level information to analysis that can support management decisions: organize the data, test how costs behave, investigate variances, and communicate the financial picture clearly.
