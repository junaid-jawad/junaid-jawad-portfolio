01 / OVERVIEW

How can raw job-costing data help explain product and regional performance?

This project brought together revenue, gross margin, direct material, direct labor, and manufacturing overhead to evaluate financial trends and cost discrepancies.

02 / APPROACH

From information to insight.

  1. 01

    Organize the data

    Used Excel formulas and PivotTables to analyze job-costing data across products and geographic regions.

  2. 02

    Investigate cost differences

    Compared applied and actual manufacturing overhead and examined direct-material price and efficiency variances.

  3. 03

    Interpret performance

    Evaluated contribution margins and break-even performance to connect accounting measures with management decisions.

03 / ANALYSIS

What the analysis covered.

Areas covered in this project
Product & regional performanceRevenue, gross margin, direct material, direct labor, and manufacturing overhead.
Cost behavior & variancesApplied versus actual overhead, direct-material price variances, and efficiency variances.
Decision supportContribution margins, break-even analysis, and identification of financial trends.
