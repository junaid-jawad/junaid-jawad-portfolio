PROJECT 02 / MANAGERIAL ACCOUNTING

Cost-Volume-Profit
Modeling.

A two-product-line Excel model used to explore how contribution margins, sales mix, and changing costs affect break-even points and projected profit.

CONTEXTIntro to Managerial Accounting
WHENSpring 2026
METHODSExcel modeling, CVP, scenario analysis
THE PROJECT / 02

The question

How do shifts in supplier costs, sales mix, and contract terms affect profitability? The model examined two product lines to make those tradeoffs easier to evaluate.

What I did

  • Built an Excel-based cost-volume-profit model covering contribution margins, break-even points, target-profit volumes, and projected operating income.
  • Evaluated scenarios involving supplier cost increases, changes in sales mix, and alternative contract terms.
  • Compared outcomes using weighted-average contribution margins, margin of safety, and pro forma income statements.

What it demonstrates

The project connects accounting measures to practical planning. By changing assumptions and comparing the resulting financial picture, I could assess how operating choices influence profit and support a reasoned recommendation.
