PROJECT 02 / FINANCIAL MODELING

Cost-Volume-Profit
Modeling.

A two-product-line Excel model for evaluating how costs, sales mix, and contract terms influence break-even points and projected operating income.

01 / OVERVIEW

How do changes in operating assumptions affect profitability?

The model connected sales volume and cost assumptions to contribution margins, target-profit volumes, and projected operating income across two product lines.

02 / APPROACH

From information to insight.

  1. 01

    Establish the model

    Built an Excel-based cost-volume-profit model covering contribution margins, break-even points, target-profit volumes, and projected operating income.

  2. 02

    Evaluate scenarios

    Examined supplier cost increases, changes in sales mix, and alternative contract terms to understand their impact on profitability.

  3. 03

    Compare financial outcomes

    Used weighted-average contribution margins, margin of safety, and pro forma income statements to support management recommendations.
